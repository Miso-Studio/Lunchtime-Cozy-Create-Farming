# Welcome to Lunchtime 🌿

Hey, welcome to Lunchtime!

This is a cozy Minecraft pack about farming, cooking, building, Create, exploring and playing together. You don't have to rush through anything. Make a little farm, build a kitchen, start a project with friends or just see where the world takes you.

Use this guide when you need help finding something or figuring out how a part of the pack works.

## New here?

Install the pack, make a world and have a look at the in-game questbook. Start with whatever sounds fun. You can farm, cook, build, explore or work on a Create project.

There's no one way to play Lunchtime. Follow the quests or ignore them for a while. It's your world.

## What can you do?

- **🌱 Farm & cook** — grow ingredients, raise animals and make food.
- **⚙️ Create & automate** — build machines, farms and production lines.
- **🏡 Build & decorate** — make a home, café, workshop or whole town.
- **📜 Follow quests** — Ember's Questing gives you things to explore and work toward.
- **🌍 Explore** — find new places, structures and resources.
- **🤝 Play together** — share builds, trade, cook and work on projects with friends.
