## Welcome to Lunchtime!

Lunchtime is for the little things that make a Minecraft world feel like home. Grow your own food, cook something nice, decorate your place, build Create machines, explore and work on a world together.

There's no rush. Build your home, start a farm, make a factory, follow the quests or just hang out with friends.

## What can you do?

- **Farm & cook** — grow ingredients and make meals, snacks and baked goods.
- **Build & decorate** — make a cozy home, kitchen, farm or a whole town.
- **Create & automate** — build machines and contraptions to help with everyday tasks.
- **Explore & progress** — discover new places, collect things and work through quests.
- **Play together** — build, trade, cook and explore with friends.
