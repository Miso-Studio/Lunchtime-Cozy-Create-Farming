## About Lunchtime

Lunchtime Cozy Create and Cooking is a relaxed Minecraft modpack for 1.20.1 Forge. Grow crops, cook all kinds of dishes, automate your farm with Create, build a cozy home and trade with friends using a real coin economy. There's no rush and no wrong way to play. Follow the questbook, pick a job, or just build a café by the river.

The pack is made for single-player and small multiplayer groups, and it's tuned to run well on mid-range PCs.

## Features

- **Farming and cooking:** Farmer's Delight, the Let's Do series, the Kaleidoscope cooking mods, Brewin' and Chewin', Pizza Delight, HerbalBrews and more. Lunchtime's Little Cookbook shows every food and how to make it.
- **Create automation:** Create with Steam 'n' Rails, Slice & Dice, Central Kitchen, Enchantment Industry, Copycats+, Liquid Fuel and Pantographs & Wires for farms, factories and trains.
- **Jobs and economy:** pick a career with Miso Jobs & Careers (Farmer, Miner, Chef or Rancher) and trade with Create: Numismatics coins.
- **Quests and guides:** an Ember's Questing questbook and Miso's Little Guide, with tutorials from Miso, the pack's mascot.
- **Building and decorating:** Supplementaries, Another Furniture, Chipped, Every Compat, Effortless Building, Fairy Lights and many furniture and decoration mods.
- **Exploration:** new structures from MVS, Integrated Dungeons and Structures, Explorify and YUNG's mods, plus new biomes, caves and End and Nether content.
- **Bosses:** L_Ender's Cataclysm bosses, with double health to make them a real challenge.
- **Multiplayer:** Open Parties and Claims, Simple Voice Chat, Snail Mail and e4mc for hosting a world for friends without port forwarding.
- **Calm nights:** no phantoms, fewer hostile mobs, longer days with Gentle Seasons, and new wildlife from Naturalist.

See the [full mod list](MODLIST.md) for all 346 mods.

## How to install

1. Install the [CurseForge app](https://www.curseforge.com/download/app).
2. Open the [Lunchtime page on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/lunch-but) and click **Install**.
3. In the profile settings, set memory to **8 GB**.
4. Click **Play**. The first launch takes a few minutes.

Coming from v1.5? Start a new world, or back up your old one first. v2 replaces quests, jobs, claims, graves and several mobs, so old worlds lose data.

## FAQ

**Is Lunchtime good for low-end PCs?**
It runs well on mid-range PCs with 8 GB allocated. On weaker PCs, turn shaders off and lower the render distance. The [performance guide](PERFORMANCE_GUIDE.md) has the details.

**Can I play with friends?**
Yes. Host your world with e4mc (no port forwarding), or run a dedicated server. See [Multiplayer and servers](docs/multiplayer-and-servers.md).

**Does it have quests?**
Yes. Ember's Questing guides you through farming, cooking, Create and exploration. The quests are optional.

**Are there shaders?**
Complementary Reimagined, Complementary Unbound and Photon are included. Pick one under Video Settings → Shader Packs.
